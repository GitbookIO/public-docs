# AI credits

GitBook's AI features run on AI credits. Each paid site includes a monthly allowance, and you can add a credit pack if you need more.

AI pricing is outcome-based. You're only charged for results you get from an AI feature, not for every request.

## Credits included with your plan

| Site plan | Credits included per site, per month |
| --------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Essential | 2,000                                |
| Ultimate  | 10,000                               |

## What uses credits

| Feature                    | Credits used                          | Available on           |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| AI Assistant Ask           | 20 credits per relevant question      | Essential and Ultimate |
| AI Assistant Chat          | 20 credits per relevant question      | Ultimate               |
| Auto-updating translations | 30 credits per 1,000 words translated | Essential and Ultimate |

## Credit packs

If your included credits aren't enough, you can buy a credit pack in advance. Larger packs are discounted. For current pack sizes and prices, see our [pricing page](https://www.gitbook.com/pricing).

Credit packs are recurring and billed monthly. If your organization is on an annual plan, credit packs and usage are still charged every month. In your renewal month, site and member charges, usage, and your credit pack all appear on the same invoice.

## Running out of credits

When you run out of credits, usage of AI features can be auto-paused. This is something you can manage from the billing page. To keep using credits, add a credit pack.

## Legacy Translations add-on

The Translations add-on is only available to organizations on legacy pricing. On current plans, auto-updating translations use AI credits instead. See [Legacy pricing](legacy-plans.md) for more.
