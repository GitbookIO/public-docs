# GitBook AI

GitBook AI covers two distinct products, plus how your published docs work with readers' own AI tools:

* **[GitBook Agent](agent/README.md)** is your AI teammate — it writes, edits, reviews, translates, and audits your documentation.
* **[GitBook Assistant](assistant/README.md)** answers your readers' questions on your published site, using your docs as its knowledge base.
* **[AI search](ai-search.md)** gives readers quick, AI-powered answers directly in the search bar.
* **[Your docs, readers' AI](readers-ai/README.md)** covers making your published docs easy for readers' own AI tools (LLMs, MCP clients) to consume.

Not sure which is which? See [Agent vs. Assistant](agent-vs-assistant.md).
