# AI search on your site

Help readers find what they need faster with knowledge-discovery tools for your published content.

### Choose your site's search experience

GitBook sites offer different search experiences:

* **Keyword search** — a standard keyword-based experience, enabled automatically on all sites.
* **GitBook AI search** — readers get short answers to questions directly from the search box. Available on Premium and Ultimate plans.
* **GitBook Assistant** — an advanced, interactive chat experience with GitBook's AI agent. Available on the Ultimate plan. See [GitBook Assistant](assistant/README.md).

To choose, open **Customize**, under **Tools** in the site sidebar, click **AI Assistant**, and pick your preferred experience.

{% hint style="warning" %}
When GitBook Assistant is enabled, AI search is disabled. Standard keyword search always shows results in the search bar regardless of which experience you choose.
{% endhint %}

### Searching published documentation

Readers open the **Ask or search…** bar with ⌘+K (Mac) or Ctrl+K (PC), search by keyword, and jump straight to specific pages or sections across the whole site. If your site has multiple [sections](../publishing/site-sections-and-variants/README.md), results include pages from all of them.

### GitBook AI search

{% hint style="info" %}
Available on Premium and Ultimate plans.
{% endhint %}

GitBook AI search gives basic AI-powered answers in the **Ask or search…** bar. It's trained on your docs site's content, but can't pull from external sources.

Once enabled, readers ask a question directly in the bar — opened by clicking it, or with ⌘+K / Ctrl+K. Alongside a summarized answer, they see an expandable section showing the sources used, plus related follow-up questions.

{% hint style="warning" %}
GitBook AI search doesn't work across separately [published sections](../publishing/site-sections-and-variants/README.md) on different docs sites — multi-section search only works within sections that live in the same site.
{% endhint %}
