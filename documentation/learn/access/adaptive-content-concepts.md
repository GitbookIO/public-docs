# Adaptive content: how claims flow

{% hint style="info" %}
Adaptive content is available on the Ultimate plan.
{% endhint %}

When a visitor reaches your site, you may already know things about them — who they are, which plan they're subscribed to, which features they can access. Adaptive content builds a tailored documentation experience around that.

{% hint style="info" %}
Adaptive content is slightly different from [authenticated access](authenticated-access/README.md), although the two can work together. Authenticated access protects your docs behind a login; adaptive content customizes published material based on data from various sources — including authenticated access, or your own app.
{% endhint %}

### How it works

Adaptive content works one of two ways:

1. Passing data from your app to GitBook.
2. Passing data from authenticated access.

The data a visitor brings with them is called their **claims** — data that helps identify who they are. You control which claims exist, and use them in the GitBook editor to show or hide pages, variants, and sections in your docs.

See [Configure adaptive content](configure-adaptive-content.md) to start setting it up.
