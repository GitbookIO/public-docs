# Track with Google Analytics

Connect the Google Analytics integration from a site's **Integrations** menu to track your published docs alongside your other properties, in addition to GitBook's own built-in [site analytics](README.md).

### The cookies notice

Once Google Analytics is connected, your site can show visitors a cookies notice — GitBook's site analytics uses cookies to identify returning visitors and gather the data needed for [insights](insights/README.md), and enabling Google Analytics adds Google's own tracking on top.

To remove the notice, open **Site settings → Integrations**, and disable or remove **Google Analytics**.

{% hint style="warning" %}
Disabling these cookies also disables GitBook's own [site analytics](README.md) for that site.
{% endhint %}
