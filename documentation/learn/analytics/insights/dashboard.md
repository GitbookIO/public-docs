# Insights dashboard

The AI insights dashboard gives you a snapshot of your site through four key metrics:

* **Savings** — estimated effort saved through questions GitBook Assistant answered.
* **Questions** — the unique questions visitors asked.
* **Answered** — the percentage of questions successfully addressed.
* **Topics** — your content's insights, sorted by shared themes.

Click any topic to open its detail view — see [Browse questions by topic](browse-by-topic.md).
