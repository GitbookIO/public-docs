# Site analytics and AI insights

{% hint style="info" %}
AI insights is available on the Ultimate plan.
{% endhint %}

AI insights shows what your visitors are asking GitBook Assistant, and how effectively it answers using your content. Open it by clicking **Insights** from your site's overview.

* [Insights dashboard](dashboard.md) — the four key metrics at a glance.
* [Browse questions by topic](browse-by-topic.md) — see how themes in visitor questions perform over time.
* [Review how a question was answered](review-an-answer.md) — inspect an individual answer and its sources.
* [Identify content gaps](identify-content-gaps.md) — find where your docs fall short.
* [Fix gaps with GitBook Agent](fix-gaps-with-agent.md) — turn a gap into a fix.

### How do I use AI insights?

AI insights gives your team an overview of how visitors interact with your documentation while searching for answers. Filter it to find:

* The questions visitors ask most often.
* Questions visitors search for that don't have answers.
* Topics your documentation doesn't cover.

Addressing these gaps helps visitors find answers faster, and understand your product more quickly.
