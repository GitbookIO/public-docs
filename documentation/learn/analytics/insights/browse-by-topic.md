# Browse questions by topic

The **Topics** view shows how individual topics perform over time, and lists the questions related to each one.

### Questions in this topic

GitBook groups a topic's questions by **Type** or **Recency** — use this to review recurring needs, spot documentation gaps, and understand whether your site answers questions in that area successfully.

Click any question to open its detail view — see [Review how a question was answered](review-an-answer.md).
