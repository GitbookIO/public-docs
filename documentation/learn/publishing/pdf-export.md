# PDF export

### Allow readers to export a PDF of your published content

{% hint style="info" %}
Available on Premium and Ultimate plans.
{% endhint %}

To enable or disable PDF export for visitors to your [published docs site](README.md), open **Customize**, under **Tools** in the site sidebar, and go to **Configure → Page actions**. Toggle **Export as PDF** on or off — this controls whether readers of your published content can download it as a PDF.

### Export your own internal content as PDF

Separately, any logged-in member of your organization can export a page — or an entire section — from your internal knowledge base as a PDF file.

{% hint style="warning" %}
Links across sections aren't currently supported when exporting internal content to PDF.
{% endhint %}

#### Export an individual page

1. Open the page, then open its Actions menu next to the page title.
2. Select **Export to PDF → Current page**.
3. Once the page loads, click **Print or save as PDF** in the upper right to open your browser's print menu.
4. Save as a PDF, or open it in your PDF viewer, using your browser's usual process.

#### Export an entire section

1. Open the [section header](../../resources/the-gitbook-interface.md)'s Actions menu and choose **Export as PDF** from the dropdown.

   {% hint style="info" %}
   This action isn't available from within a change request.
   {% endhint %}
2. Once the page loads, click **Print or save as PDF** in the upper right to open your browser's print menu.
3. Save as a PDF, or open it in your PDF viewer, using your browser's usual process.
